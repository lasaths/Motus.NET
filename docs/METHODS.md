# Methods catalog

Motus.NET keeps method provenance close to API contracts. Units are explicit at the call surface: joint coordinates use each `JointLimit.Unit` (`Radians` or `Meters`), TCP positions are meters, orientations are quaternions with tolerances in radians, and times are seconds.

| Area | API | Method / status | Units and failure surface | References |
|------|-----|-----------------|---------------------------|------------|
| URDF / xacro import | `UrdfRobotLoader.LoadXacro`, `LoadTreeXacro` | Minimal in-process xacro subset: include, property, simple macro/call, `${arg}` substitution. `$(find)` is rejected. | URDF meters/radians; `NotSupportedException` for `$(find)`, `InvalidOperationException` for malformed trees. | `docs/urdf-import.md` |
| Joint LIN | `JointLinearPlanner` | Joint-linear interpolation with optional collision validation. | Joint coordinates follow `JointLimit.Unit`; collision returns `planning.path_collision`/endpoint codes. | Deterministic managed implementation. |
| Cartesian LIN | `CartesianLinearPathPlanner` | TCP-linear interpolation with IK at samples and optional collision/constraint validation. | TCP meters, orientation radians; IK failures are explicit LIN status strings. | Deterministic managed implementation. |
| PoE FK/IK | `ProductOfExponentials`, `PoEJacobian`, `NumericalInverseKinematics` | Modern Robotics Product of Exponentials and body-Jacobian numerical IK for URDF serial chains. | Revolute radians, prismatic meters; `NumericalIkFailureReasons` names non-convergence/singularity/invalid input. | Lynch & Park, *Modern Robotics*. |
| TOTG | `RetimerAlgorithm.Totg`, `TrajectoryRetimer` | Managed TOPP-RA-style retiming using path parameter `s`, backward controllable squared-velocity intervals, and forward greedy acceleration under separable joint limits. | Velocity/acceleration read from `JointLimit` in that axis unit; invalid NaN/Inf or non-positive limits throw named exceptions. | `TotgMethodRefs`, Pham & Pham 2018, DOI `10.1109/TRO.2018.2819195`. |
| Constraints | `PathConstraints`, `PositionConstraint`, `OrientationConstraint`, `IConstraintChecker` | MoveIt-shaped TCP validity checks without ROS dependency. Planners evaluate TCP frames via FK where available. | Position meters, orientation radians; failures carry `planning.constraint_violation` and `ConstraintViolation: ...` reasons. | `ConstraintMethodRefs`, Sucan et al. 2012 OMPL seam prior art, DOI `10.1109/MRA.2012.2205651`. |
| RRT-Connect | `SamplingPlannerId.RrtConnect`, `ManagedRrtConnect` | Bidirectional RRT-Connect managed fallback; native OMPL path optional when available. | Joint coordinates follow `JointLimit.Unit`; collision/constraint endpoints and segments rejected with structured planning messages. | Kuffner & LaValle, ICRA 2000; OMPL when native. |
| PRM* | `SamplingPlannerId.PrmStar`, `ManagedPrmStar` | Managed PRM* roadmap with seeded sampling and radius `gamma(log n / n)^(1/d)`, Dijkstra extraction, existing collision checker reuse. | Joint coordinates follow `JointLimit.Unit`; iteration/time caps and seeded `Random` for reproducibility. | `PlanningMethodRefs`, Karaman & Frazzoli 2011, DOI `10.1177/0278364911406761`. |
| CHOMP-lite | `SamplingPlannerId.ChompSmooth`, `ChompLiteSmoother` | Managed post-process smoother with finite-difference smoothness and binary collision/constraint penalty checks. | Joint coordinates follow `JointLimit.Unit`; accepted moves must keep adjacent segments valid. | `PlanningMethodRefs`, Zucker et al. 2013, DOI `10.1177/0278364913488805`; TrajOpt/GPMP2 are documented alternatives for future optimizer stacks. |
| Stewart/Gough | `StewartPlatform`, `StewartRobot`, `Stewart*Kinematics` | Parallel platform leg-length IK/FK stack. | `RobotPreset.Family = "stewart"`; six leg coordinates are meters, stroke/singularity/FK divergence are structured reasons. | Motus ADR / component docs. |
| Legged preview | `LegIk3R`, `LeggedLayout`, `LeggedGait` | Analytic 3R leg IK and duty-cycle gait preview with static-stability checks. | `RobotPreset.Family = "legged"`; leg joints are radians, terrain/body positions meters. | `LeggedMethodRefs`: Lynch & Park; Song & Waldron DOI; McGhee & Frank DOI; Bretl & Lall DOI. |
